Krypton (Kr)

Krypton is a noble gas with atomic number 36, in period 4 and group 18 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 83.798 u and its electron configuration is [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² 4p⁶. Gas at 25 °C.
