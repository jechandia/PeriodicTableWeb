Ytterbium (Yb)

Ytterbium is a lanthanide with atomic number 70, in period 6, in the f-block of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 173.045 u and its electron configuration is [Xe] 4f¹⁴ 6s². Solid at 25 °C.
