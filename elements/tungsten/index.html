Tungsten (W)

Tungsten is a transition metal with atomic number 74, in period 6 and group 6 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 183.84 u and its electron configuration is [Xe] 4f¹⁴ 5d⁴ 6s². Solid at 25 °C.
