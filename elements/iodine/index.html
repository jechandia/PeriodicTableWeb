Iodine (I)

Iodine is a halogen with atomic number 53, in period 5 and group 17 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 126.90447 u and its electron configuration is [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s² 5p⁵. Solid at 25 °C.
