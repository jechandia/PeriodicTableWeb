Xenon (Xe)

Xenon is a noble gas with atomic number 54, in period 5 and group 18 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 131.293 u and its electron configuration is [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s² 5p⁶. Gas at 25 °C.
