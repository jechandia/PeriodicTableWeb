Hafnium (Hf)

Hafnium is a transition metal with atomic number 72, in period 6 and group 4 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 178.486 u and its electron configuration is [Xe] 4f¹⁴ 5d² 6s². Solid at 25 °C.
