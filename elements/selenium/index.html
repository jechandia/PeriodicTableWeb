Selenium (Se)

Selenium is a nonmetal with atomic number 34, in period 4 and group 16 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 78.971 u and its electron configuration is [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² 4p⁴. Solid at 25 °C.
