Cerium (Ce)

Cerium is a lanthanide with atomic number 58, in period 6, in the f-block of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 140.116 u and its electron configuration is [Xe] 4f¹ 5d¹ 6s². Solid at 25 °C.
