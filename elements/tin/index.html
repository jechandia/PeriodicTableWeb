Tin (Sn)

Tin is a post-transition metal with atomic number 50, in period 5 and group 14 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 118.71 u and its electron configuration is [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s² 5p². Solid at 25 °C.
