Indium (In)

Indium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 49, in period 5 and group 13 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 114.818 u and its electron configuration is [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s² 5p¹. Solid at 25 °C.
