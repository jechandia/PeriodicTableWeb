Platinum (Pt)

Platinum is a transition metal with atomic number 78, in period 6 and group 10 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 195.084 u and its electron configuration is [Xe] 4f¹⁴ 5d⁹ 6s¹. Solid at 25 °C.
