Ruthenium (Ru)

Ruthenium is a transition metal with atomic number 44, in period 5 and group 8 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 101.07 u and its electron configuration is [Kr] 4d⁷ 5s¹. Solid at 25 °C.
