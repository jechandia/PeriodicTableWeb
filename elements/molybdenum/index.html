Molybdenum (Mo)

Molybdenum is a transition metal with atomic number 42, in period 5 and group 6 of the periodic table. Its atomic mass is 95.95 u and its electron configuration is [Kr] 4d⁵ 5s¹. Solid at 25 °C.
